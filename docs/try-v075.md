# Try this (room_lab v0.75): a 2-minute tour of what's new

Everything below is **off until you turn it on**, and you can turn each part off again the same way.

**Opening settings:** hold the back of your left wrist toward your face for half a second. The SETTINGS panel opens.
- If you've turned on the shoulder button, it's under shoulder button → SETTINGS instead.
- Each switch is a button: green means on, and pressing it toggles it.

## 1. Settings in categories (SETCAT), 15 s

1. In SETTINGS, press **SETCAT** (under PANEL).
2. The panel shrinks to a short **MOST USED** row and seven category buttons. Press **LOOK**.
3. **What good looks like:**
   - The LOOK switches open in a second panel just beside the first.
   - Greyed buttons are switches that are off for a reason; the reason is written under them, and you can still press them.
   - **BACK** closes the side panel.
4. **To turn it off:** go to LOOK → SETCAT, or use the full list.

## 2. Steadier pointer (STEADY), 15 s

1. Go to INPUT → **STEADY**.
2. Point at a screen and hold your hand as still as you can.
3. **What good looks like:**
   - The cursor stops trembling, but still follows you when you move on purpose.
   - A pinch-click lands where the cursor was, even if your hand jerks as you pinch.
4. **To turn it off:** INPUT → STEADY again.

## 3. Controls always visible (CTLTOP), 15 s

1. Go to SCREENS → **CTLTOP**.
2. Look at a screen that sits low, near the floor.
3. **What good looks like:**
   - The white move bar under it is visible and grabbable, never hidden by the floor or the room mesh.
   - If there's no room for the bar under the screen, it becomes a tab on the screen's bottom edge.
4. **To turn it off:** SCREENS → CTLTOP.

## 4. Live clean-up while scanning (CLEANLIVE), 40 s

1. Go to SCAN AND ROOM. Turn on **SCAN**, and then **CLEANLIVE**.
2. Start a scan: shoulder button or wrist ring → **SCAN**. Look around the room slowly.
3. **What good looks like:**
   - Straight, clean walls appear over the raw scan: gold where Meta knows the wall, green where only the scan saw it.
   - Each room's outline is drawn in blue on the floor.
   - A line in your view tells you what's missing and where, for example:
     - **NOT YET: WALL BEHIND YOU UNSEEN**: turn around and look at it.
     - **NOT YET: DOORWAY TO YOUR LEFT UNCLEAR**: look at the doorway.
     - **ROOM: GOOD** (green): you're done. Press **DONE**.
   - After DONE, the clean walls become where screens can be placed.
4. **To turn it off:** SCAN AND ROOM → CLEANLIVE. The scan goes back to raw walls.

## 5. Drawing on the walls (WALLS), 30 s

1. Go to SCAN AND ROOM → **WALLS**. This also turns the wall tool on. Press **LINE**.
2. Follow the hint line at the bottom of your view:
   1. Pinch where the line starts.
   2. Point where it ends. That can be on another wall, even across the room: the dashed line bends around the corners.
   3. Pinch to keep it.
3. Then try:
   - **EXPAND**: pull the line out from the wall into a surface, then pinch.
   - **CIRCLE**: pinch the centre, then pinch again to set the size.
4. **What good looks like:**
   - A hint is always shown, and **UNDO** always steps back one thing.
   - When there's nothing left to undo, it says **NOTHING TO UNDO**.
   - Your shapes are still there after a restart.
5. **To turn it off:** SCAN AND ROOM → WALLTOOL.

## 6. Smoother room mesh (MESHBATCH), 10 s

1. Turn on **GEOMETRY** (the room wire mesh) to see it, then SCAN AND ROOM → **MESHBATCH**.
2. **What good looks like:**
   - The mesh looks exactly the same.
   - The frame rate holds up better with the mesh on: the headset's status reports fewer draw calls.
3. **To turn it off:** SCAN AND ROOM → MESHBATCH.

## If something looks wrong

Turn that one switch off: everything else keeps working. Then tell work-df what you saw. A rough "the wall hint said
X but the wall was right there" is plenty.
