# Try this (room_lab v0.53)

Settings live on the wrist panel or under the shoulder button → SETTINGS. Every switch has a one-line explanation.

- **Claude on a screen (ASSIST, on):** pinch the terracotta button at a screen's top-right corner. The chat opens and
  says hi. Talk, then pause; Claude answers in the chat and listens again. Pinch the button again to close the chat.
- **Scroll (SCROLL, under EXPERIMENTAL):** turn it on. Point at a screen, open your hand flat with the palm facing away
  from you, and move it up/down or left/right; the page follows your hand.
- **Draw on walls (WALLTOOL, under EXPERIMENTAL):** turn it on, then shoulder button → WALLS → LINE. Pinch point A on a
  wall, then point B, then CONFIRM. EXPAND pulls the line out into a surface. CIRCLE works the same way.
- **Move or close a screen:** hold the bar under any screen for half a second, point at a wall, and let go. To close
  one: SETTINGS → turn LOCK off, then pinch the X in the screen's corner twice.
- **Scan a room (SCAN + GEOMETRY):** shoulder button → SCAN, then look around slowly. Walls fill in as calm panels;
  press DONE. The scanned walls can then hold screens, even in rooms Meta never set up.
